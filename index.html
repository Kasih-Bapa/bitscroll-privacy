Bitscroll Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Auto Agent is a private publishing tool. The person who runs Bitscroll uses it to post Bitscroll's own videos and captions to the Bitscroll Facebook Page, the Bitscroll Instagram account and the operator's own Threads account. Members of the public cannot sign in to it or use it.

What the tool stores

The tool stores the access tokens and account IDs for those accounts, in a private file on the operator's own computer. It uses them only to publish posts and read basic information about those accounts.

What the tool does not do

The tool does not collect personal information from people who watch Bitscroll videos or visit this page. It does not sell data, share data with advertisers, or use data for advertising.

What Meta receives

Each post sends a video and its caption to Meta so that Meta can publish it. Meta's own privacy policy covers how Meta handles its platforms and your activity on them.

How long data is kept

Tokens and account IDs stay on the operator's computer until they are replaced or the tool's access is removed.

Deleting data

The tool holds no data about anyone other than the operator of Bitscroll. If you have a question or want to request deletion of anything you believe relates to you, use the contact link below and we will respond.
