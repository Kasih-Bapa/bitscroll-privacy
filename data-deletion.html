Bitscroll Data Deletion

Last updated 9 November 2026

Auto Agent is a private publishing tool used only by the operator of Bitscroll. It stores access tokens and account IDs for the operator's own accounts. It holds no data about anyone else. See the privacy policy.

Remove the app's access

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings and privacy, then Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites.
  3. Find Auto Agent and choose Remove.

Ask us to delete data

Open a request on GitHub and say what you want deleted. Do not post private details in the request, because requests there are public. We will delete anything held about you and confirm in the same request.
